Self-hosted keys only

There is no hosted wallet, no browser extension, and no “connect account” flow. A form requesting a seed phrase is theft. This site will not provide one.

Keys remain in the bitfucd descriptor wallet on the operator’s machine.

bitfuc-cli -regtest createwallet miner
bitfuc-cli -regtest -rpcwallet=miner getnewaddress
bitfuc-cli -regtest -rpcwallet=miner getbalance
bitfuc-cli -regtest -rpcwallet=miner sendtoaddress <fucrt1…> 10

Use backupwallet when the material matters. Do not transmit backups to this host.
