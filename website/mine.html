Document 02 · Mining

Block production

You mine on a node you run. This page does not produce blocks and does not pay anyone. Nothing public is live.

If you have already built the node on your computer, ./scripts/ui/start.sh opens a local helper (buttons, not a hosted wallet). Coins stay on that machine until a public network exists.

What is ready in the source tree

The node already speaks getblocktemplate and submitblock. A small solo miner lives at contrib/bitfuc/solo_mine.py. Local setup: scripts/mine/regtest-setup.sh. Full notes: docs/mining.md.

./scripts/mine/regtest-setup.sh
python3 contrib/bitfuc/solo_mine.py --chain regtest --address fucrt1… --blocks 1

Addresses use fucrt1 on regtest and tfuc1 on the public test chain (NO VALUE), not Bitcoin bcrt1 / bc1.

What is not ready

There is no public peer list, no pool, and no mainnet. Coinbase is spendable after 100 further blocks. Laptop SHA-256d is for development; it is not a claim about ASIC-scale security.
