How the system is constructed

BITFUC reuses Bitcoin Core’s consensus kernel. This website does not reimplement secp256k1, Script, or the UTXO set.

Operator or miner → bitfuc-cli / getblocktemplate → bitfucd → P2P (BITFUC magic and ports only), validation, chainstate, mempool, descriptor wallet.

Identity

Genesis, magic bytes, ports, bech32 HRPs (fuc / tfuc / fucrt), Base58 versions, datadir, and user-agent are independent of Bitcoin. A Bitcoin node will not follow this chain.

Open parameters

Public proof-of-work, difficulty adjustment, and a frozen mainnet schedule remain listed in docs/open-decisions.md. They are not implied by this page. Regtest uses SHA-256d with a trivial target so generatetoaddress works on ordinary hardware.
