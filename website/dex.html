No book, no liquidity

There is currently no meaningful liquidity. No order book, no automated market maker, no listed pair, no reported volume.

A later design note may compare a native book, FUC↔BTC atomic swaps, and why an Uniswap replica is not a UTXO exchange. Implementation is not scheduled on this page.

A control offering instantaneous “swaps” on this hostname would be unauthorized. None is provided.
